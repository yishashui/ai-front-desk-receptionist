# Welcome message

Spoken first, as soon as the call connects (AI speaks first, pause before speaking: 0s).

```
Thank you for calling Sunrise PACE Center. For English, press 1. 普通话请按 2。廣東話請按 3。
```

- Press 1 → English
- Press 2 → Mandarin
- Press 3 → transfer to a Cantonese-speaking staff member
- No response → the AI repeats the menu in Mandarin, word for word
